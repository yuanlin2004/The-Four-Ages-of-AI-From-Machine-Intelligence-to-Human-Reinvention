# The Four Ages of AI: How Humanity Lost—and Rediscovered—Itself

**An essay on what happens to people, institutions and self-understanding when intelligence stops being scarce.** Formerly subtitled *From Machine Intelligence to Human Reinvention*.

**[Read the website](https://rooagi.github.io/The-Four-Ages-of-AI-From-Machine-Intelligence-to-Human-Reinvention/)**

## Chapters

1. [Age I · The Mirror](docs/age-1-mirror.md) — the crisis of authenticity. *What is real?*
2. [Age II · The Surrender](docs/age-2-surrender.md) — the erosion of agency. *Who decides?*
3. [Age III · The Irrelevance](docs/age-3-irrelevance.md) — the crisis of purpose. *Why do we matter?*
4. [Age IV · The Becoming](docs/age-4-becoming.md) — the reinvention of humanity. *What shall we become?*
5. [Synthesis · Human Reinvention](docs/human-reinvention.md) — what the four ages have in common, and what is still ours to decide.

## Join the Conversation

This essay is an invitation to think together about the future of AI—not a final prediction. The four ages are a starting framework, and thoughtful disagreement is welcome.

**What would you change?** Is authenticity really in crisis, or are we adapting? When does delegating to an AI expand our agency, and when does it erode it? What should give people standing if their skills stop being scarce? Which futures for the fourth age are missing?

- **[Share an idea or start a discussion](https://github.com/RooAGI/The-Four-Ages-of-AI-From-Machine-Intelligence-to-Human-Reinvention/issues/new)** — challenge a thesis, suggest a new perspective, or raise a question.
- **[Explore ongoing conversations](https://github.com/RooAGI/The-Four-Ages-of-AI-From-Machine-Intelligence-to-Human-Reinvention/issues)** — read and respond to others' ideas.
- **[Propose an edit or additional perspective](https://github.com/RooAGI/The-Four-Ages-of-AI-From-Machine-Intelligence-to-Human-Reinvention/pulls)** — contribute improvements, references, counterarguments, or additional analysis through a pull request.

Contributions are especially valuable when they offer clear reasoning, concrete examples, evidence, or constructive counterarguments. Our goal is to deepen the conversation rather than reach premature consensus.

## Context

The four ages are an interpretive framework, not an inevitable sequence. They overlap, may arrive in a different order, and will unfold differently across societies. The dates attached to them are scenario labels, not forecasts, and the opening scenes in each chapter are invented and illustrative.
