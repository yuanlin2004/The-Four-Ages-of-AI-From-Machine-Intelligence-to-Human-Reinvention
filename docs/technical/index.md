# Technical companions

*How the machines work, for readers who want the mechanism behind the human story*

The four ages in this essay are social ages. Each one is driven by a real change in what AI systems can do, and these companions explain those changes in more depth than the main chapters need to. They began as the essay's original four chapters, and their research and citations are preserved here largely as written.

| Companion | What it explains | Where it matters in the essay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Architecture and the Transformer transition](architecture-and-the-transformer.md) | How deep learning moved from many specialized architectures to one reusable foundation | [Age I · The Mirror](../age-1-mirror.md) |
| [Scaling, parallel computation and inference](scaling-and-parallel-computation.md) | Why large language models scaled, why memory movement matters, and why inference is still hard | [Age I · The Mirror](../age-1-mirror.md), [Age II · The Surrender](../age-2-surrender.md) |
| [Agents, software and trust](agents-and-trust.md) | Personal and cloud agents, generated software, and the security boundary around delegated action | [Age II · The Surrender](../age-2-surrender.md), [Age III · The Irrelevance](../age-3-irrelevance.md) |
| [AI for science, mathematics and new computation](ai-for-science.md) | Machine-assisted discovery in mathematics and physics, and its limits | [Age IV · The Becoming](../age-4-becoming.md) |

!!! info "Two timelines, one essay"
    The technical history and the social history do not line up one-to-one. The pre-2017 "age of alchemy" was a technical period; *The Mirror* describes a social shift that began later, once fluent machine expression became cheap and widely available. The companions keep the technical periodization because it is accurate for the technology. The essay's four ages are about people.
